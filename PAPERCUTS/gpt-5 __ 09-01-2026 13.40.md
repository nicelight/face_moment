# Papercuts

- `Dockerfile` copies `src/` before building the application wheel, so each
  source-only change invalidates the wheel layer and re-resolves/downloads the
  full pinned dependency set. During TASK-088, both the RED-source and
  GREEN-source image builds downloaded large scientific wheels; this made a
  small query/test feedback cycle take several minutes. No Dockerfile change
  was in the task scope.
