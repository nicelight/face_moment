# EP-001 — Fresh Searchable Inventory

## Value

An authenticated photographer can independently admit fresh JPEG photographs
under a selected СПА/date and observe them become searchable in time for the
participant Promo journey. Authorized users can also observe recent processing
activity and safely hide, restore or permanently purge commercial-photo
inventory within their role.

## Features

- [FT-001](../features/FT-001.md): Independent photographer photo admission.
- [FT-002](../features/FT-002.md): Compatible background processing and
  searchable readiness.
- [FT-012](../features/FT-012.md): Role-scoped Photo Inventory Operations and
  recent per-СПА queue statistics.

## Success Metrics

- At least 95% of independently accepted unique JPEGs become searchable in less
  than 15 minutes from their server-side `photo.accepted_at`.
- Same-СПА/date checksum duplicates create no additional `photo_id`, pipeline
  state, search result, teaser or contribution to `N`.
- Per-СПА 1/5/60-minute `new`, `unprocessed`, `processed` and `failed`
  counters follow their accepted definitions and refresh every five seconds.
- Soft-deleted Photos leave new search/result formation and statistics while
  already issued sessions continue using their media. A confirmed global hard
  purge rejects restore of snapshot members until completion, retains Promo
  sessions/core Attempts/diagnostic evidence and lets clients skip removed
  media.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The photographer selects one СПА and authoritative `visit_date`; every HTTPS
  upload reports its own accepted, rejected or duplicate outcome without a
  group confirmation step.
- Each unique Photo and its serving-pipeline `pending` state are committed
  atomically per photo.
- Every accepted unique file exposes a truthful current processing/searchable
  state, including distinct `no_faces` and `failed` outcomes.
- Only compatible `ready` pipeline state is searchable, and at-least-once work
  does not duplicate final face records.
- Queued work survives backend/worker restart; unfinished `processing`
  work returns to `pending` and resumes from the beginning.
- Metric population and timing follow PRD FR-ING-08 without excluding late
  `no_faces`, `failed`, `pending` or `processing` items.
- A photographer can soft-delete/restore only their own Photos; an
  operator/developer can do so for any authorized Photo using СПА,
  authoritative `visit_date` and the effective capture-time range.
- Restore preserves Photo data and processing state; soft deletion excludes the
  Photo from new search/result formation and all queue-statistics counters but
  does not invalidate an already issued session.
- Project-wide restore-all and confirmed hard purge cover all soft-deleted
  Photos. Purge waits for the shared worker, shows waiting/progress state,
  rejects restore of fixed-snapshot members, resumes after restart and adds no
  per-photo purge lifecycle or jobs table.
- Hard purge removes Photo-owned media/face/pipeline data while retaining Promo
  results/sessions, core Attempts and diagnostic evidence; clients skip missing
  media without recalculating `N`.
