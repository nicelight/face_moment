# EP-001 — Fresh Searchable Inventory

## Value

An authenticated photographer can turn a confirmed, authoritative batch of
fresh JPEG photographs into a transparent searchable inventory in time for the
participant Promo journey.

## Features

- [FT-001](../features/FT-001.md): Photographer batch ingest and immutable
  confirmation.
- [FT-002](../features/FT-002.md): Compatible background processing and
  searchable readiness.

## Success Metrics

- At least 95% of unique accepted JPEGs in confirmed pilot manifests become
  searchable in less than 15 minutes from `batch.confirmed_at`.
- Cross-batch same-СПА/date duplicates create no additional accepted manifest
  member, `photo_id`, object, job, search result, teaser or contribution to `N`.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The photographer completes authenticated upload, validation, review and
  immutable confirmation for one СПА and authoritative `visit_date`.
- Every accepted unique file exposes a truthful current processing/searchable
  state, including distinct `no_faces` and `failed` outcomes.
- Only compatible `ready` pipeline state is searchable, and at-least-once work
  does not duplicate final face records.
- Metric population and timing follow PRD FR-ING-08 without excluding late
  `no_faces`, `failed`, `pending` or `processing` items.
