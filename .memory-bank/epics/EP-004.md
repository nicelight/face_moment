# EP-004 — Публичный поиск и покупка фотографий

## Value

Посетитель без регистрации находит фотографии по своему селфи в выбранных
площадках, получает одну цену и скачивает выбранные originals бесплатно или
после оплаты. Operator/developer управляют бесплатностью и единым тарифом.

## Features

- [FT-013](../features/FT-013.md): verified camera/profile search and ordered personal/common gallery.
- [FT-014](../features/FT-014.md): verified combined selection, global quote and venue/tariff administration.
- [FT-015](../features/FT-015.md): verified free-original HTTP/browser/ZIP/bearer delivery without a provider.
- [FT-016](../features/FT-016.md): one cross-venue paid purchase and confirmed-original delivery.

## Success criteria

Bounded search, retained captures, >=320 px previews, correct marginal RUB quote,
no payment before archive ready, server-gated originals, bearer ready+3 days and
admin email on archive failure are checked by the feature AC below. No new
latency/conversion/recognition-accuracy promise is added.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Discovery/profile branches and public scope close through FT-013-AC-001..009.
- Selection/price/admin access close through FT-014-AC-001..006.
- Free delivery/preparation failure/expiry close through FT-015-AC-001..004.
- Paid order/receipt/payment confirmation/expiry close through FT-016-AC-001..005.
- No registration, advanced identity protection, second provider or guaranteed
  retention shielding is introduced; historical Promo/QR contracts are preserved.
