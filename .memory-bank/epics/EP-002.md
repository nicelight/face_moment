# EP-002 — Automatic Promo and QR Continuation

## Value

A pilot participant is detected without taking an action, sees four relevant
professional-photo teasers on the exit display, and continues the same
personalized result on a phone with one QR scan and no new selfie.

## Features

- [FT-003](../features/FT-003.md): Sensor-triggered reference capture and
  attempt control.
- [FT-004](../features/FT-004.md): Scoped realtime face search and group-result
  assembly.
- [FT-005](../features/FT-005.md): Promo presentation and degraded advertising
  fallback.
- [FT-006](../features/FT-006.md): QR phone continuation and personalized-session
  expiry.

## Success Metrics

- At least 19 of the same 20 controlled attempts show four correct unique
  teasers and a fully visible, scannable QR in less than 10 seconds from
  `reference_series_ready_at`.
- Every completed phone continuation preserves the same СПА, authoritative
  `visit_date`, available teaser and issued `N`; hard-purged media is skipped.
- Expired personalized sessions reveal no teaser, `N` or other result data.

## Acceptance Criteria

- A passage event starts one fresh bounded capture/search attempt while overlap
  and stale-result replacement are prevented.
- Search uses compatible revision, СПА, operator-selected active date, quality
  gate and calibrated threshold before any candidate enters result assembly.
- Success contains exactly four unique valid teasers and computes `N` from the
  complete valid unique-photo union; full group-member coverage is not claimed.
- Failure returns to local advertising without final Promo or success cooldown.
- Server-communication failure also shows the small non-blocking
  `Попытка связи с сервером была не успешна в hh:mm:ss` notice for 5–10
  seconds; newer notice may replace it.
- QR continues the issued session without selfie and obeys independent display,
  first-open and browser-idle lifetimes. Soft delete does not invalidate an
  issued session; hard-purged media is skipped without changing its issued `N`.
