# EP-002 — Automatic Promo and QR Continuation

## Value

A pilot participant is detected without taking an action, sees four relevant
professional-photo teasers on the exit display, and continues the same
personalized result on a phone with one QR scan and no new selfie.

## Features

- [FT-003](../features/FT-003.md): Sensor-triggered reference capture and
  attempt control.
- [FT-004](../features/FT-004.md): Scoped realtime face search and group-result
  assembly.
- [FT-005](../features/FT-005.md): Promo presentation and degraded advertising
  fallback.
- [FT-006](../features/FT-006.md): QR phone continuation and personalized-session
  expiry.

## Success Metrics

- At least 19 of the same 20 controlled attempts satisfy the independent
  server-correctness gate, and at least 19 satisfy the independent fully
  visible/scannable QR-under-10-seconds gate from `reference_series_ready_at`.
  The two counts are reported separately; a joint intersection is not required.
- Every completed phone continuation preserves the same СПА, authoritative
  `visit_date`, available teaser and issued `N`; hard-purged media is skipped.
- Expired personalized sessions reveal no teaser, `N` or other result data.
- Central server roles start and operate without the display session; Chromium
  restarts automatically after browser failure when the central origin is
  reachable and returns to local advertising.
- Realtime admits one inference owner under one deadline, returns typed `busy`
  to a concurrent admitted request without a waiter queue and never replays
  unfinished reference work after restart.
- The browser/intact-volume recovery procedure can be followed and verified by
  an authorized operator within the pilot's accepted recovery limits.

## Acceptance Criteria

- A passage event starts one fresh bounded capture/search attempt while overlap
  and stale-result replacement are prevented.
- The central-origin Chromium client receives the local ESP32 event through one
  authenticated 10-second mDNS HTTP long-poll at a time, traverses the
  reference series chronologically and submits the first at most 20 BlazeFace
  occurrences in one synchronous multipart request.
- Search uses compatible revision, СПА, operator-selected active date, quality
  gate and calibrated threshold before any candidate enters result assembly.
- Success contains exactly four unique valid teasers and computes `N` from the
  complete valid unique-photo union; full group-member coverage is not claimed.
- Failure returns to local advertising without final Promo or success cooldown.
- Central runtime independence, automatic Chromium recovery, singleton
  admission with typed `busy`, and restart interruption without replay are
  directly observable through the owning feature AC.
- An operator can execute the documented browser and intact-volume restart
  recovery procedure and confirm the named readiness/advertising checks.
- Server-communication failure also shows the small non-blocking
  `Попытка связи с сервером была не успешна в hh:mm:ss` notice for 5–10
  seconds; newer notice may replace it.
- QR continues the issued session without selfie and obeys independent display,
  first-open and browser-idle lifetimes. Soft delete does not invalidate an
  issued session; hard-purged media is skipped without changing its issued `N`.

Feature-level acceptance closure is owned by `FT-003-AC-001..017`,
`FT-003-AC-019..020`, `FT-004-AC-001..008`, `FT-005-AC-001..005` and
`FT-006-AC-001..005`, each with its governing `REQ-*` and verification method.
