# FT-009 — Developer Log Explorer

**Epic:** [EP-003 — Explainable Diagnostics and Calibration](../epics/EP-003.md)

## Outcome

An authorized developer can search structured browser/server logs globally and
navigate from a relevant record to its correlated attempt without direct
browser access to PostgreSQL or impact on the participant-critical flow.

## Requirements

- `REQ-LOG-001`.
- Cross-cutting: `REQ-DATA-001`, `REQ-ARCH-001`.
- Registry: [.memory-bank/requirements.md](../requirements.md).

## Use Cases

- Filter structured logs by time, source, component, severity, release, message
  and correlation fields.
- Open a related attempt from a correlated log record.
- Investigate browser/server events that are not usefully discoverable from an
  Attempts-first workflow.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Log Explorer supports every PRD-required search field and navigation from a
  correlated record to its attempt.
- Search operates through the existing backend and PostgreSQL; the browser does
  not connect to PostgreSQL directly.
- Browser/server log emission remains non-blocking for capture, search, Promo
  and QR.
- Log records exclude images, embeddings, auth headers, cookies, tokens, request
  bodies and session replay.
- Technical log records expire after 30 days.

## Edge and Failure Behavior

- Diagnostic ingestion failure does not block a critical participant flow and
  leaves its evidence gap observable.
- A structured event without an applicable attempt correlation remains
  searchable without inventing an attempt association.
- Expired log data cannot be recovered through stale result links.
