# FT-005 — Promo Presentation

**Epic:** [EP-002 — Automatic Promo and QR Continuation](../epics/EP-002.md)

## Outcome

The exit display moves from local advertising to a truthful, readable and
scannable four-photo Promo only for a complete valid result, then returns safely
to advertising according to an independent display lifetime.

## Requirements

- `REQ-UX-001`, `REQ-UX-004`, `REQ-PERF-001`, `REQ-REL-001`.
- Cross-cutting: `REQ-ARCH-001`.
- Registry: [.memory-bank/requirements.md](../requirements.md).

## Use Cases

- Show local advertising between attempts and a non-personal prePromo while
  capture/search is active.
- Present four low-quality no-watermark teasers plus the session QR on success.
- Return to local advertising after result duration or any non-success path.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Promo shows exactly four unique teaser photos on the 43-inch landscape
  16:9/1920x1080 baseline.
- QR is fully visible, high-contrast, programmatically decodable and scannable on
  representative real phones in the target setup.
- Copy is exactly `Ваши фотографии найдены — откройте по QR-коду` and does not
  claim pilot download availability.
- At least 19 of 20 controlled attempts reach a fully visible/scannable QR in
  less than 10 seconds from `reference_series_ready_at` as part of the combined
  Promo flow.
- Display success is confirmed only by an idempotent acknowledgement after all
  four teasers decode and QR is fully visible. Missing acknowledgement after the
  display window is derived as `unconfirmed` without scheduler machinery.
- Result-display expiry returns to advertising without invalidating an otherwise
  live QR session.

## Edge and Failure Behavior

- Partial, stale or fewer-than-four results never enter final Promo or Chime.
- Missing optional sound/animation uses a silent, non-blocking fallback.
- Camera, network or processing failure leaves or returns the client to local
  advertising and does not start success cooldown.
- Server-communication failure additionally shows
  `Попытка связи с сервером была не успешна в hh:mm:ss` for 5–10
  seconds; a newer notice may replace it immediately.
