# FT-005 — Promo Presentation

**Epic:** [EP-002 — Automatic Promo and QR Continuation](../epics/EP-002.md)

## Outcome

The exit display moves from local advertising to a truthful, readable and
scannable four-photo Promo only for a complete valid result, then returns safely
to advertising according to an independent display lifetime.

## Requirements

- `REQ-UX-001`, `REQ-UX-003`, `REQ-UX-004`, `REQ-PERF-001`,
  `REQ-REL-001`.
- Cross-cutting: `REQ-SEC-001`, `REQ-ARCH-001`.
- Registry: [.memory-bank/requirements.md](../requirements.md).

## Use Cases

- Show local advertising between attempts and a non-personal prePromo while
  capture/search is active.
- Present four low-quality no-watermark teasers plus the session QR on success.
- Return to local advertising after result duration or any non-success path.

## Acceptance Criteria

### FT-005-AC-001 — Complete truthful Promo

- REQ: REQ-UX-001
- Observable criterion: Only a complete result displays exactly four unique
  low-quality no-watermark teasers on the 43-inch landscape 16:9/1920x1080
  baseline, a fully visible high-contrast QR and the exact copy
  `Ваши фотографии найдены — откройте по QR-коду` without a download claim.
- Development-closure verification: render complete and incomplete fixtures at
  the logical target baseline, using four distinct disposable teaser fixtures;
  inspect teaser identity/copy/layout and programmatically decode the QR.
- Post-deployment pilot validation: repeat the same proof on the target screen
  and scan the issued QR with representative real phones when the server,
  hardware and real photo corpus exist. This pilot proof is deferred evidence,
  not a development-closure gate before that environment exists.

### FT-005-AC-002 — Independent visible-QR performance gate

- REQ: REQ-PERF-001, REQ-UX-001
- Observable criterion: At least 19 of the same 20 controlled production
  attempts make the QR fully visible and scannable in less than 10 seconds from
  `reference_series_ready_at` on one client monotonic clock, including local
  processing and request sending. This count is independent of FT-004's
  server-correctness count; no joint pass set is required.
- Development-closure verification: prove the one-clock marker wiring and
  deterministic request/render timing with controlled disposable fixtures.
- Post-deployment pilot validation: use instrumented client markers and physical
  scan evidence for the same 20 attempts and report the independent QR/latency
  count without excluding no-match/timeout. This is deferred until the deployed
  server, target hardware and real pilot corpus are available.

### FT-005-AC-003 — Post-render display acknowledgement

- REQ: REQ-UX-001, REQ-SEC-001
- Observable criterion: Display success becomes `confirmed` only after a
  token-scoped idempotent acknowledgement sent when all four teasers are
  decoded and QR is fully visible; render failure may report `failed`, and
  absence of confirmation after the display window is derived as terminal
  `unconfirmed` without a scheduler or acknowledgement outbox.
- Verification: Exercise duplicate/late acknowledgement, render failure and no-
  acknowledgement paths and inspect the resulting display status.

### FT-005-AC-004 — Independent display expiry

- REQ: REQ-UX-003
- Observable criterion: Result-display expiry returns the display to local
  advertising without invalidating an otherwise active QR session.
- Verification: Let the display duration expire, then open/read the same QR
  session within its independent validity window.

### FT-005-AC-005 — Non-success advertising fallback

- REQ: REQ-UX-004, REQ-REL-001
- Observable criterion: Partial, stale, fewer-than-four, camera, network and
  processing failures never show final Promo/Chime or start success cooldown;
  local advertising remains/returns usable, optional asset loss is silent, and
  server-communication failure shows the accepted replaceable 5–10-second
  non-blocking timestamp notice.
- Verification: Inject each named failure/asset condition, assert display and
  cooldown state, and time/replace the communication notice.

## Edge and Failure Behavior

- Partial, stale or fewer-than-four results never enter final Promo or Chime.
  Covered by `FT-005-AC-005`.
- Missing optional sound/animation uses a silent, non-blocking fallback.
  Covered by `FT-005-AC-005`.
- Camera, network or processing failure leaves or returns the client to local
  advertising and does not start success cooldown. Covered by
  `FT-005-AC-005`.
- Server-communication failure additionally shows
  `Попытка связи с сервером была не успешна в hh:mm:ss` for 5–10
  seconds; a newer notice may replace it immediately. Covered by
  `FT-005-AC-005`.

## Clarifications

### 2026-08-26 — Scoped development-stage provider-edge checkpoint

- For the current scoped recovery run, the only accepted intermediate outcome
  is the independently verified Promo provider API/edge repair: zero direct
  provider queries, no forbidden provider imports and authenticated SPA scope.
  The evidence is retained in
  `.tasks/TASK-076-T3-FT-005-W1/attempt-2-verifier-evidence.md` and
  `.tasks/TASK-076-T3-FT-005-W1/attempt-2-provider-edge-green.md`.
- QR physical-scan proof and distinct real four-photo/no-watermark proof are
  unavailable at this development stage because the test machine has no real
  photos before ingest/processing is complete. They are deferred to
  post-deployment pilot validation. Development closure must still use four
  distinct issued disposable references and programmatic QR decoding; a shared
  disposable placeholder may support local rendering but cannot prove distinct
  real media or no-watermark content.
- The canonical pilot proof remains required once the relevant
  ingest/processing inputs, deployed server and authorized physical scan are
  available; its absence before that point does not keep the implementation
  feature open.
- At that checkpoint no product target, REQ, AC ID, SDD design or canonical
  display contract changed. The existing planning surface had to distinguish
  development closure proof from deferred post-deployment pilot validation;
  that evidence-timing decision remains, subject to the later independent-gate
  clarification below.

### 2026-08-26 — Development closure versus post-deployment pilot validation

- Operator decision: real target-screen, representative-phone, real-photo and
  controlled 20-attempt pilot checks must not block development closure while
  the deployed server, target hardware and real photo corpus do not exist.
- These checks remain product/pilot validation obligations and are deferred;
  this decision does not claim that the live pilot has passed.
- Development closure uses deterministic, isolated evidence: distinct
  issued disposable references, programmatic QR decoding, logical target-size
  browser rendering, API/edge checks and failure-path probes. A shared
  disposable placeholder may support local rendering, but must not be used to
  claim four distinct real teasers or no-watermark pilot content.
- The direct provider API/edge boundary remains a development blocker. This
  clarification changes evidence timing, not ownership, public behavior or the
  no-bypass architecture rule.
- The task-plan and testing-spec owners must encode the same two-phase split so
  `/verify` does not return `NEEDS-CLARIFICATION` solely because deferred
  pilot hardware/data are unavailable.

### 2026-08-29 — Independent QR/latency gate

- Operator decision: FT-005-AC-002 is an independent production gate over the
  same 20 controlled attempts. At least 19 attempts must pass the visible,
  scannable QR and under-10-second one-clock check.
- The gate does not filter on FT-004 server correctness and does not calculate a
  joint intersection. FT-004 and FT-005 report separate 19/20 counts while
  retaining their existing ownership boundaries.
