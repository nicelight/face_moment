# FT-010 — Ground-truth Annotation

**Epic:** [EP-003 — Explainable Diagnostics and Calibration](../epics/EP-003.md)

## Outcome

An authorized developer can add simple person/detection-level ground truth to
an attempt so group-search behavior and Calibration calculations have explicit,
reviewable correct, false and missed evidence.

## Requirements

- `REQ-ANN-001`.
- Cross-cutting: `REQ-DATA-001`, `REQ-ARCH-001`.
- Registry: [.memory-bank/requirements.md](../requirements.md).

## Use Cases

- Enter a real pilot-participant name within an authorized diagnostic annotation.
- Mark detection/result semantics as correct, wrong/false or missed.
- Reuse the annotation for attempt analysis and Calibration input.

## Acceptance Criteria

### FT-010-AC-001 — Calculation-ready ground truth

- REQ: REQ-ANN-001
- Observable criterion: An authorized developer attaches a participant name and
  correct, false or missed semantics to the relevant Attempt/person/detection
  evidence in a form usable by threshold and individual quality-gate
  calculations.
- Verification: Create each outcome on representative evidence and trace it
  into the immutable calculation-ready projection with a stable Attempt
  locator. FT-011 owns proof that Calibration consumes this projection and
  exposes contributing-Attempt drill-down.

### FT-010-AC-002 — Annotation privacy boundary

- REQ: REQ-ANN-001, REQ-DATA-001
- Observable criterion: Participant names and annotations are visible only in
  authorized developer diagnostics and never enter general technical logs.
- Verification: Exercise developer/operator/photographer authorization and scan
  structured logs, URLs and general Attempt projections for names.

### FT-010-AC-003 — Explicit missing and missed semantics

- REQ: REQ-ANN-001
- Observable criterion: Missing annotation is excluded from calculations that
  require ground truth instead of becoming a result class, while `missed`
  remains recordable without requiring proof of local-detector misses or a
  reference-frame-upload mode.
- Verification: Compare calculation inputs for absent and explicit `missed`
  annotations and confirm no mandatory detector-miss artifact dependency.

### FT-010-AC-004 — Ordinary annotation retention

- REQ: REQ-DATA-001
- Observable criterion: Ordinary annotation/evidence expires with its 90-day
  context without using annotation creation time as a separate cutoff.
- Verification: Apply the ordinary Attempt cutoff to annotation fixtures and
  confirm that expired rows are unavailable.

### FT-010-AC-005 — Promoted annotation subset

- REQ: REQ-DATA-001
- Observable criterion: Only the exact curated promoted annotation subset,
  including its entered name, may remain until explicit whole-subset deletion.
- Verification: Promote selected and unselected annotation fixtures, inspect
  surviving fields, delete the whole promoted subset and repeat the delete once.

## Edge and Failure Behavior

- Missing annotation keeps the case out of calculations that require ground
  truth rather than being interpreted as a result category. Covered by
  `FT-010-AC-003`.
- `missed` annotation semantics do not require proof of local-detector misses
  or a diagnostic reference-frame upload mode. Covered by `FT-010-AC-003`.
- Ordinary annotation/evidence data expires with its 90-day context. Covered by
  `FT-010-AC-004`.
- A manually promoted Calibration case retains only its exact curated
  annotation subset until explicit whole-subset deletion. Covered by
  `FT-010-AC-005`.

The pilot does not add a participant registry, dataset catalog or embedded
test-management platform.

## Clarifications

### 2026-09-04 — Cross-feature proof ownership

FT-010 ends at an immutable calculation-ready annotation projection carrying a
stable Attempt locator. FT-011 proves Calibration calculations and
contributing-Attempt drill-down. This clarification narrows verification
ownership without changing behavior or adding an acceptance criterion.

### 2026-09-04 — Atomic retention ownership

The former AC-004 combined ordinary expiration and promoted-subset retention,
which are independently implementable operations. Ordinary expiration keeps
the stable AC-004 ID; the promoted-subset half is AC-005. No accepted behavior
or production scope was added.

### 2026-09-04 — Minimal deletion ownership

TASK-098 implements the diagnostics-owned whole-promoted-subset deletion
required by AC-005 because the baseline has no such operation. It is idempotent
and internal; no public or annotation-specific deletion route is added.

## SDD Design Gate

Complete. The linked subject specs define normalized ordinary annotations,
developer-only HTML mutation, missing-versus-missed calculation input and the
separate ordinary-retention and promoted-snapshot boundaries. Verification does
not require local-detector miss proof.
