# FT-002 — Processing and Searchable Readiness

**Epic:** [EP-001 — Fresh Searchable Inventory](../epics/EP-001.md)

## Outcome

Every unique accepted photo progresses through compatible, idempotent background
processing to a truthful searchable or terminal failure/no-face state, with the
pilot ingest SLO measured over the complete defined population.

## Requirements

- `REQ-ING-003`, `REQ-ING-004`, `REQ-SRCH-001`, `REQ-REL-002`,
  `REQ-SEC-001`.
- Cross-cutting: `REQ-ARCH-001`.
- Registry: [.memory-bank/requirements.md](../requirements.md).

## Use Cases

- Photographer observes per-photo processing and searchable status.
- Operator observes photo readiness, failures and storage/recovery health.
- Background processing creates previews and pipeline-specific face records for
  the selected serving revision.

## Acceptance Criteria

### FT-002-AC-001 — Truthful compatible processing state

- REQ: REQ-ING-003, REQ-ING-004, REQ-SRCH-001
- Observable criterion: Every committed Photo begins with its atomic
  serving-pipeline `pending` state and progresses for one immutable compatible
  revision through `processing` to exactly one current terminal outcome:
  `ready`, `no_faces` or `failed`; only compatible `ready` is searchable. An
  ordinary serving-revision switch from current A to B MUST be rejected while
  any Photo admitted against A remains `pending` or `processing` in its A
  state. A terminal A state of `ready`, `no_faces` or `failed` does not block
  the switch; a rejected switch leaves A committed.
- Verification: Attempt an ordinary A-to-B switch with an A-admitted Photo in
  each non-terminal state and assert rejection plus unchanged committed A.
  Drive Photos through each A terminal path, assert that each terminal state
  no longer blocks the switch, then assert UI/API, persisted state and
  exact-search eligibility against the selected revision.

### FT-002-AC-002 — Idempotent at-least-once completion

- REQ: REQ-SRCH-001, REQ-REL-002
- Observable criterion: Repeating processing for the same
  `(photo_id, pipeline_revision_id)` converges on one terminal derivative/face
  set and never duplicates final face records.
- Verification: Inject retry after derivative publication and before terminal
  commit, then compare resulting media, face rows and state with a single run.

### FT-002-AC-003 — Durable restart recovery

- REQ: REQ-REL-002
- Observable criterion: Backend/worker restart preserves the full
  `pending|processing` population; unfinished `processing` returns to `pending`
  and restarts from the beginning without losing accepted work.
- Verification: Restart during active and queued work and assert preserved
  population, reset transition and eventual idempotent terminal outcomes.

### FT-002-AC-004 — Full-population searchable SLO

- REQ: REQ-ING-004
- Observable criterion: At least 95% of independently accepted unique JPEGs
  become searchable in less than 15 minutes from `photo.accepted_at`; late
  `pending`, `processing`, `failed` and `no_faces` remain breaches, while
  rejects and duplicates remain outside the denominator. Each accepted Photo
  is classified exactly once through the state for its immutable
  admission-time serving revision; later revision states neither
  replace nor add a denominator member. A requested interval is half-open
  `[accepted_from, accepted_before)`; an empty population returns zero counts
  with no ratio and no pass/fail verdict.
- Verification: Compute the metric from persisted acceptance/state timestamps
  over a controlled population, both interval boundaries and an empty interval,
  and reconcile every included and excluded item.

### FT-002-AC-005 — Shared-worker delay remains explicit

- REQ: REQ-ING-004, REQ-ARCH-001
- Observable criterion: An explicitly developer-triggered Calibration run may
  delay photo processing during debugging, but the delayed population and SLO
  effect remain visible and no priority/preemption scheduler is introduced.
- Verification: Occupy the shared worker with Calibration, inspect queued Photo
  visibility and metric accounting, then confirm ordinary processing resumes.

### FT-002-AC-006 — Processing and storage health visibility

- REQ: REQ-REL-002, REQ-SEC-001
- Observable criterion: Authorized operational views expose processing
  failures/recovery state and configured primary PostgreSQL/MinIO free capacity
  separately enough to diagnose readiness risk while the stores and internal
  ports remain private and the capacity/status surface discloses no protected
  credentials, authentication state, storage/model paths, object keys,
  embeddings, mounted data content or commercial media.
- Verification: Query health with normal and injected low-capacity/failure
  conditions, inspect the effective private/read-only topology and assert
  distinct current values plus the required response/URL/log redaction.

### FT-002-AC-007 — Native SFace Photo path

- REQ: REQ-SRCH-001
- Observable criterion: An eligible `opencv_sface` revision processes a Photo
  through its configured YuNet detection, `FaceRecognizerSF.alignCrop` and
  SFace recognition path, validates its model/revision identity and embedding
  dimension, and never consumes Buffalo M bbox, landmark, crop or alignment
  output.
- Verification: Run one deterministic synthetic Photo through the configured
  SFace adapter and record the native call path, accepted revision/dimension,
  normalized embedding and rejection of mismatched or Buffalo-derived input.

### FT-002-AC-008 — Native Buffalo M Photo path

- REQ: REQ-SRCH-001
- Observable criterion: An eligible `insightface_buffalo_m` revision processes
  a Photo through its configured SCRFD, native landmark/alignment and Buffalo M
  `normed_embedding` path, validates its model/revision identity and embedding
  dimension, and never consumes OpenCV SFace bbox, landmark, crop or alignment
  output.
- Verification: Run one deterministic synthetic Photo through the configured
  Buffalo M adapter and record the native call path, accepted revision/
  dimension, normalized embedding and rejection of mismatched or SFace-derived
  input.

### FT-002-AC-009 — Buffalo readiness requires native inference

- REQ: REQ-SRCH-001, REQ-ARCH-001
- Observable criterion: The configured Buffalo adapter remains unready until
  its native detector and recognizer both complete synthetic inference with
  valid native output and revision-compatible embedding dimension. A dynamic
  detector input is configured before use; successful warmup is followed by
  working Photo processing. Native loading or warmup failure keeps readiness
  closed through both serving and Calibration admission without fallback.
- Verification: Use the actual local `scrfd.onnx` and `w600k_r50.onnx` with an
  in-memory matching revision to reproduce the dynamic-input defect and prove
  warmup plus processing of a 320-by-320 synthetic image. Record both native
  inference calls, readiness transitions and finite normalized 512-dimensional
  recognizer output; inject independent native detector/recognizer failures to
  prove existing admission rejection. Skipped native tests or only detector
  doubles do not close this criterion. This is audit finding 5's corrective
  delta, not reassignment of the original AC-008/initial-startup proof.

### FT-002-AC-010 — Versioned original-pixel SFace Photo preprocessing

- REQ: REQ-SRCH-001
- Observable criterion: The new explicit preprocessing revision detects
  photographer Photos at long-edge 640 (and the experimental 1280 pass), without
  upscaling, duplicate dimensions or tiling. Boxes and all five landmarks map
  through actual resize ratios to the EXIF-oriented original; duplicate
  cross-scale faces merge without merging neighbours. SFace aligns original
  pixels into its native 112x112 input. Old revision behavior, stored originals,
  native Buffalo and single-pass query crops remain isolated.
- Verification: Deterministic non-square/rounding, EXIF, small-image, duplicate,
  neighbouring-face and image-edge fixtures prove original-coordinate terminal
  results and original-pixel alignCrop; compare old revision and query call
  traces and test serving/Calibration adapter admission.

### FT-002-AC-011 — Evidence-based Photo scale selection

- REQ: REQ-SRCH-001, REQ-ARCH-001
- Observable criterion: Compare 640 against 640+1280 on the six available
  portraits and available group images, retaining per-image detections,
  matching observations and timing. Choose 640 unless the second scale adds
  useful verified detections or matching improvement. Approximately 7% face
  size and groups up to eight define investigation scope, never rejection or
  detection caps. Report sample limitations and missing labels/camera evidence;
  no universal accuracy target or invented human ground truth is required.
- Verification: Repeat native CPU inference on unchanged originals with the
  same models and query inputs, inspect overlays and record the selection with
  observed gain/cost and the available evidence's limits.

### FT-002-AC-012 — Local revision application preserves accepted inventory

- REQ: REQ-SRCH-001, REQ-ING-003, REQ-REL-002
- Observable criterion: Apply the selected explicit revision to the authorized
  workstation testing runtime and reprocess existing Photos through processing
  and serving-control boundaries. Originals, Photo IDs/checksums/visibility,
  admission revision and old revision outcomes remain intact. Worker/realtime
  bind the new committed revision; new state reaches truthful terminal outcomes
  and compatible search reads the new ready faces. Repeating the operation
  never resets terminal states or duplicates rows. No remote deployment occurs.
- Verification: Snapshot local inventory/original hashes and old states, apply
  through the guarded serving command and processing-owned pending creation,
  restart model consumers, observe every selected Photo and compare preserved
  data plus repeated-run counts. Prove failure/retry behavior in disposable
  state, not by destructive mutation of the workstation inventory.

## Edge and Failure Behavior

- An ordinary serving-revision switch from A to B is rejected before B commits
  while any Photo admitted against A has an A state of `pending` or
  `processing`. A `ready`, `no_faces` or `failed` A state does not block it.
  Model comparison remains test-only and is separately owned by FT-011.
  Covered by `FT-002-AC-001`.
- `pending`, `processing`, `failed` and `no_faces` after 15 minutes remain SLO
  breaches; they are not silently removed from the denominator. Covered by
  `FT-002-AC-004`.
- Rejected candidates and checksum duplicates create no Photo and are outside
  the metric population. A later non-admission revision state, whether current
  serving or not, is not selected, while its accepted Photo remains exactly
  once through the immutable admission-revision state. Covered by
  `FT-002-AC-004`.
- Developer-triggered Calibration may delay the shared worker and this metric
  during debugging without adding scheduling machinery. Covered by
  `FT-002-AC-005`.
- Primary-storage capacity and photo-processing recovery gaps remain
  observable without weakening private topology or disclosing protected data.
  Covered by `FT-002-AC-003` and `FT-002-AC-006`.

### 2026-08-14 — Ordinary serving-revision switch terminal guard

- Operator answer: an ordinary serving-revision switch A-to-B MUST be rejected
  while any Photo admitted against current A remains `pending` or
  `processing` in its A state. Terminal `ready`, `no_faces` and `failed` A
  states do not block. Model comparison is test-only and remains separately
  handled by FT-011.
- Consequence: `FT-002-AC-001` now owns the observable rejection/terminal
  progression rule without changing its stable ID or governing REQ linkage.
  The edge behavior states the same rule, including the FT-011 boundary.
- Design impact: the decision changes the shared serving-control/processing
  switch contract. Its planning impact is bounded to FT-002; canonical
  reconciliation is required through `/spec-redesign`.
- Behavior spec impact: none; FT-002 has no linked behavior JSON.
