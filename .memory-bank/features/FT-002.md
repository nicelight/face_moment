# FT-002 — Processing and Searchable Readiness

**Epic:** [EP-001 — Fresh Searchable Inventory](../epics/EP-001.md)

## Outcome

Every unique accepted photo progresses through compatible, idempotent background
processing to a truthful searchable or terminal failure/no-face state, with the
pilot ingest SLO measured over the complete defined population.

## Requirements

- `REQ-ING-003`, `REQ-ING-004`, `REQ-SRCH-001`, `REQ-REL-002`.
- Cross-cutting: `REQ-ARCH-001`.
- Registry: [.memory-bank/requirements.md](../requirements.md).

## Use Cases

- Photographer observes per-photo processing and searchable status.
- Operator observes batch readiness, failures and storage/recovery health.
- Background processing creates previews and pipeline-specific face records for
  the selected serving revision.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Original storage, photo record and serving work are created idempotently.
- `(photo_id, pipeline_revision_id)` progresses through `pending`, `processing`
  and exactly one current terminal outcome: `ready`, `no_faces` or `failed`.
- Only compatible `ready` state is searchable; native pipeline processing and
  immutable revision isolation are preserved.
- At-least-once execution does not duplicate final face records.
- At least 95% of all unique accepted JPEGs in confirmed manifests become
  searchable in less than 15 minutes from `batch.confirmed_at`.

## Edge and Failure Behavior

- `pending`, `processing`, `failed` and `no_faces` after 15 minutes remain SLO
  breaches; they are not silently removed from the denominator.
- Pre-confirmation rejects, checksum duplicates and non-serving jobs are not in
  that metric population.
- Capacity, cleanup and recovery gaps remain observable.
