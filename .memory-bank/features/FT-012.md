# FT-012 — Photo Inventory Operations

**Epic:** [EP-001 — Fresh Searchable Inventory](../epics/EP-001.md)

## Outcome

Authorized photographers and staff can find commercial Photos by venue, date
and capture-time range, hide or restore them within their role, observe recent
per-СПА processing activity, and permanently purge all soft-deleted media
through one controlled project-wide operation.

## Accepted Feature Boundary

On 2026-07-24, the operator explicitly accepted FT-012 as one planning and
completion boundary for the current pilot, covering both role-scoped
deletion/restore/global purge and recent per-СПА 1/5/60-minute counters. The
read-only counters remain part of FT-012 even though they are independently
testable. Splitting or renumbering this boundary requires a new explicit
operator decision.

## Use Cases

- A photographer selects one СПА, authoritative `visit_date` and capture-time
  range, then soft-deletes or restores only Photos uploaded by that
  photographer.
- An operator or developer soft-deletes or restores any Photo in an accessible
  СПА using the same selection scope.
- Staff view separate per-СПА `new`, `unprocessed`, `processed` and `failed`
  counters for the last 1, 5 and 60 minutes.
- An authorized operator/developer restores every soft-deleted Photo across the
  project.
- An authorized operator/developer confirms permanent deletion of every
  currently soft-deleted Photo and observes worker wait/progress until
  completion.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Selection uses one СПА, authoritative `visit_date` and effective
  `captured_at`.
- Effective `captured_at` uses reliable EXIF time in the СПА timezone, otherwise
  that file's server-side upload-start time, otherwise 01:00 on its
  authoritative `visit_date`.
- Photographer access is owner-scoped; operator/developer access may cover any
  Photo in an accessible СПА. Project-wide restore-all and hard purge remain
  authorized operator/developer admin actions.
- Soft deletion preserves the Photo, media, face data, pipeline state and other
  related data while immediately excluding the Photo from participant-facing
  search, media access and all queue-statistics counters.
- Restore reactivates preserved state without upload or reprocessing.
- `restore all soft deleted` restores every currently soft-deleted Photo in
  every СПА.
- `hard delete ALL softed media` requires confirmation and fixes its
  project-wide soft-deleted target snapshot at that confirmation.
- If the shared worker is occupied, the UI says
  `Начну удаление, как только закончится процесс {human-readable process name}`
  and purge waits without preemption.
- During purge, the destructive interface is replaced by completed/total
  progress for the fixed snapshot.
- Hard purge removes each snapshot Photo, its original/preview/thumbnail media,
  face data, pipeline state and every Promo result/session containing it. Core
  Attempts and diagnostic evidence remain under their ordinary retention rules.
- The one global purge run resumes after backend or worker restart. It adds no
  per-photo `purge_pending` lifecycle or separate purge jobs table and does not
  interrupt an upload already in progress.
- For each СПА, the 1-, 5- and 60-minute counters refresh by five-second polling:
  `new` counts active unique Photos accepted in-window by `accepted_at`;
  `unprocessed` counts active Photos accepted in-window and currently
  `pending | processing`; `processed` counts active Photos transitioned
  in-window to `ready | no_faces`; `failed` counts active Photos transitioned
  in-window to `failed`.
- Statistics use polling only; WebSocket and SSE are not required.

## Edge and Failure Behavior

- Missing or unreliable EXIF time does not create a separate manual-resolution
  path; the deterministic upload-start/01:00 fallback keeps selection usable.
- Photos soft-deleted after a purge snapshot is confirmed are not silently
  added to the running snapshot and remain available for a later purge.
- Uploads allowed to finish during purge may add ordinary durable processing
  backlog; an upload already in progress is not interrupted.
- A restart continues the same confirmed purge snapshot and does not silently
  abandon its remaining Photos.
- Removing Photo-owned and Promo session/result data does not remove core
  Attempts or diagnostic evidence, even when retained evidence contains
  historical Photo references.

## Cross-feature Boundaries

- [FT-001](FT-001.md) supplies Photo uploader identity, authoritative
  `visit_date`, upload-start timing and `accepted_at`; FT-012 owns the inventory
  action scope and effective capture-time fallback.
- [FT-002](FT-002.md) owns normal Photo processing-state transitions; FT-012
  only uses those preserved states for visibility, restore and counters.
- [FT-004](FT-004.md) and [FT-006](FT-006.md) consume only active inventory and
  must not expose a soft-deleted Photo or a purged Promo session.
- [FT-007](FT-007.md) continues to own core Attempts and diagnostic evidence;
  FT-012 hard purge explicitly retains them.
